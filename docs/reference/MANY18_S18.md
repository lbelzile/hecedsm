# ManyLab2 replication of Risen and Gilovich (2008)

The data is a replication with undergraduate students of a study. The
authors measured the likelihood of bad outcome when tempting fate by not
preparing in advance for a task. The replication data are unbalanced and
the study failed to replicate

## Usage

``` r
MANY18_S18
```

## Format

A data frame with 4433 rows and 4 variables:

- `lab`:

  \[factor\] laboratory identifier

- `likelihood`:

  \[integer\] likelihood of bad outcome, from 1 to 10

- `gender`:

  \[factor\] gender of participant, either `male` or `female`

- `condition`:

  \[factor\] experimental condition, either `prepared` or `unprepared`

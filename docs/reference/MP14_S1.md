# Maglio and Polman (2014), Study 2

Authors measured the subjective distance on travel from one subway
station (Bay) and asked the subjective distance to another station, at
most two stations away from the location of the experiment. The other
factor manipulated was the direction of travel. The data are suitable
for a two-way analysis of variance.

## Usage

``` r
MP14_S1
```

## Format

A data frame with 202 rows and 3 variables:

- `station`:

  \[factor\] subway station, one of `Spadina`, `St. George`,
  `Bloor-Yonge` and `Sherbourne`

- `direction`:

  \[factor\] direction of travel, either `east` or `west`

- `distance`:

  \[integer\] subjective distance, a Likert scale ranging from very
  close (`1`) to very far (`7`)

## Source

Open Science Foundation, <https://osf.io/7rajd/>, unspecified license

## Details

The specific question asked to commuters was "How far away does the
\[name\] station feel to you?"

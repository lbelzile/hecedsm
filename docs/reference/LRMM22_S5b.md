# Liu et al. (2019), Study 5b

The authors postulated that the underestimation of the appreciation of
initiators relative to recipients was mediated by the degree of surprise
of the recipient.

## Usage

``` r
LRMM22_S5b
```

## Format

A data frame with 402 rows and 5 variables:

- `appreciation`:

  \[double\] average score questions related to appreciation
  (appreciate, feel thankful, feel grateful, feel pleased)

- `surprise`:

  \[double\] average score for questions related to unexpectedness and
  surprise of reaching out

- `condition`:

  \[factor\] experimental condition, either `self` or `other`

- `age`:

  \[double\] age of participant

- `gender`:

  \[factor\] gender of participant, one of `male`, `female` or `other`

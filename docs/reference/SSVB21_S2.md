# van Stekelenburg et al. (2021), Study 2

The study aimed to assess changes in opinion of skeptics when faced with
evidence of scientific consensus through manipulation of experimental
condition explaining how to assess a news claim. The subject in question
was Genetically Engineered Food (GEF).

## Usage

``` r
SSVB21_S2
```

## Format

A data frame with 442 rows and 3 variables:

- `prior`:

  \[integer\] negative of prior belief score

- `post`:

  \[integer\] post experiment score for veracity of claim

- `condition`:

  \[factor\] experimental condition, one of `Boost`, `BoostPlus` and
  `consensus`

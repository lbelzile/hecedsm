# Rosen and Jerdee (1974)

Data are extracted from Table 1. They consist of assessment of bank
supervisors on the promotion of hypothetical employees to the task of
branch manager. The purpose of the study was to check discrimination for
promotion.

## Usage

``` r
RJ74
```

## Format

A data frame with 4 rows and 3 variables:

- `gender`:

  \[factor\] gender, either `male` or `female`

- `decision`:

  \[factor\] outcome, either `promote` or `hold file`

- `admin`:

  \[factor\] complexity of job, either `simple` or `complex`

- `count`:

  \[integer\] number of occurences of combination

## References

Rosen, B., and Jerdee, T. H. (1974). *Influence of sex role stereotypes
on personnel decisions.* Journal of Applied Psychology, 59(**1**), 9–14.
[doi:10.1037/h0035834](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0035834)

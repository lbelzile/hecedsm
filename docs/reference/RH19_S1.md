# Roczniewska and Higgins (2019), Study 1

Study of mediator following changes in the Polish education system,
whereby middle school was due to disappear and be replaced with a new
system with 8 years of elementary school and four years of high school.
The authors surveyed teachers in middle and primary schools with two
messages, either prevention or promotion.

## Usage

``` r
RH19_S1
```

## Format

A data frame with 198 rows and 12 variables:

- `change`:

  \[factor\] change outcome depending on school type, either middle
  school (`negative`) or primary school (`positive`)

- `regfocus`:

  \[factor\] situational regulatory focus manipulation: education change
  message framing, either `prevention` or `promotion` message framing

- `fluency`:

  \[double\] mean for fluency items

- `engagement`:

  \[double\] mean for engagement items

- `fairness`:

  \[double\] mean for change process fairness perceptions items

- `dopenness`:

  \[double\] mean for change openness items

- `promem`:

  \[double\] mean for work regulatory focus promotion items

- `prevem`:

  \[double\] mean for work regulatory focus prevention items

- `gender`:

  \[factor\] gender of teacher, either `man` or `woman`

- `age`:

  \[integer\] age of teacher

- `tenure`:

  \[integer\] number of years teaching

- `hours_week`:

  \[integer\] weekly number of hours working

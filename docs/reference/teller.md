# Teller data

Fictional data from Berger et al. (2018) discussed in Example 6.4 (First
United Federal Bank of Boston). One hunder bank tellers were offered
one-on-one training with an experienced clerk for a certain number of
weeks on the job, possibly in addition to formal training period. The
goal of the study is to determine which combination is most efficient at
reducing the monthly error in balance (in dollars).

## Usage

``` r
teller
```

## Format

A data frame with 100 rows and 3 variables:

- `course`:

  \[factor\] whether a formal training was offered (either `no` or
  `yes`)

- `nweeks`:

  \[factor\] number of weeks of training one-to-one with an experienced
  clerk

- `error`:

  \[double\] monthly error in balance account

## Source

Berger, P.D, Maurer, R.E. and G.B. Celli (2018). Experimental Design:
With Application in Management, Engineering, and the Sciences, 2nd
edition, Springer.

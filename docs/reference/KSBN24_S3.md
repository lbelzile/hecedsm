# Koetke et al. (2024), Study 3

Data for a two-way between-subject ANOVA as a function of `condition`
and `gender`.

## Usage

``` r
KSBN24_S3
```

## Format

A data frame with 369 rows and 10 variables:

- `ih`:

  \[double\] CIHS score for intellectual humility

- `condition`:

  \[factor\] experimental manipulation, either `low`, `high` or
  `neutral`

- `gender`:

  \[factor\] gender of participant, either `male`, `female` or `other`

- `gender`:

  \[factor\] experimental manipulation for gender of scientist, either
  `male` (Robert) or `female` (Sandra).

- `METI`:

  \[double\] METI scale for the trust in science

- `expertise`:

  \[double\] METI subscale for expertise

- `integrity`:

  \[double\] METI subscale for integrity

- `benevolence`:

  \[double\] METI subscale for benevolence

- `research`:

  \[double\] score for support for the research

- `intention`:

  \[double\] score for intention to follow research models

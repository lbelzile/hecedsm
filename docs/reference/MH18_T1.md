# Morgan and Halfaker (2018), Table 1

Data on the impact of Wikipedia Teahouse on the number of edits. The
authors looked at barriers to participation

## Usage

``` r
MH18_T1
```

## Format

A data frame with 12 rows and 4 variables:

- `neweditors`:

  \[integer\] number of new editors

- `period`:

  \[factor\] number of weeks since joining Wikipedia

- `group`:

  \[factor\] experimental condition, either `invited` to teahouse or
  `control`

- `edits`:

  \[factor\] level of activity, either `1-4 edits` or `5+ edits`

## References

Jonathan T. Morgan and Aaron Halfaker. 2018. Evaluating the impact of
the Wikipedia Teahouse on newcomer socialization and retention. In
*Proceedings of the 14th International Symposium on Open Collaboration
(OpenSym '18)*. Association for Computing Machinery, New York, NY, USA,
20, 1–7.

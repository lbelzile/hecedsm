# Moon and vanEpps (2023), Study 4

The authors studied the impact on donation amount of manipulating the
minimal suggested amount. The benchmark was an open-ended box, with
quantity featuring a minimum value, 15\$, 25\$ or an alternative amount.
The authors also recorded whether the person had donated before.

## Usage

``` r
MV23_S4
```

## Format

A data frame with 996 rows and 3 variables:

- `donate`:

  \[integer\] did people choose to donate to the charity? 0 for no, 1
  for yes.

- `condition`:

  \[factor\] experimental condition, either an `open-ended` amount or a
  suggested amount of `quantity1`, `quantity5` or `quantity10` for one,
  five and 10 dollars.

- `amount`:

  \[double\] amount of proposed donation, `NA` if the person declined to
  donate

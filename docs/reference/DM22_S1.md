# Dorison and Minson (2022), Study 1

Wide-format with paired observations. Participants (American citizens)
in the study were paired with people from the opposing party (Republican
or Democrat) and asked to perform a 10 minutes presentation to discuss
beliefs about the best candidate, with pointers to subjects such as
Covid19, environmental policy and immigration. Only people who
interacted via the chat are kept.

## Usage

``` r
DM22_S1
```

## Format

A data frame with 367 rows and 17 variables:

- `age`:

  \[integer\] age of participant

- `gender`:

  \[factor\] gender of participant, either `male` or `female`

- `pos_emotion_part`:

  \[integer\] partner positive emotion index

- `neg_emotion_part`:

  \[integer\] partner negative emotion index

- `wordcount_part`:

  \[integer\] partner total words in debate

- `hedges_part`:

  \[integer\] number of hedges for partners

- `pos_emotion`:

  \[integer\] positive emotion index

- `neg_emotion`:

  \[integer\] negative emotion index

- `wordcount`:

  \[integer\] total words in debate

- `hedges`:

  \[integer\] number of hedges

- `group`:

  \[factor\] group label

- `anxiety_self`:

  \[numeric\] anxiety score for self, average of four nine point Likert
  scales

- `anxiety_other`:

  \[numeric\] anxiety score for other, average of four nine point Likert
  scales

- `vote`:

  Who are you most likely to vote for in the upcoming U.S. presidential
  election (2020)? either Republican candidate (1) or Democratic
  candidate (2)

- `polideo`:

  self-reported political orientation, with choices
  very/somewhat/slightly liberal (1/2/3), neither (4), to
  slightly/somewhat/very conservative (5/6/7).
